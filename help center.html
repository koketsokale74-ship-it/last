Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?". Enter your email and we’ll send you a secure link to create a new password. If you don’t receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.
How can I report a post or user?
Click the three dots (⋯) on any post or profile and select "Report". Our moderation team will review it within 24 hours. You can also email us directly at support@gramite.com.
What are the subscription plans?
We offer Free, Pro ($9.99/mo), and Team ($29.99/mo) plans. Free includes basic features, Pro adds analytics and custom themes, Team includes collaboration tools and priority support.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps. Note: this action is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data will be removed within 30 days.
Where can I find the API documentation?
Our API docs are available at developers.gramite.com. You’ll need to generate an API key from your account settings. We support REST and GraphQL endpoints.
