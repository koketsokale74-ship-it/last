About Gramite
Gramite
Where Creativity Meets Community

Gramite is a next-generation social platform that empowers creators, connects communities, and celebrates authentic expression through short-form video and beyond.

Our Story

Gramite was born from a simple idea: to create a space where everyone can share their passion, talent, and creativity with the world. Founded in 2020, we've grown from a small team of dreamers to a global platform with millions of users.

2020

The Beginning

Gramite was founded by a group of friends who wanted to create a more authentic social media experience focused on creativity rather than popularity.

2021

First Million Users

Within a year, Gramite reached 1 million active users, proving that people were hungry for a platform that valued genuine content and community.

2022

Creator Fund Launch

We launched the Gramite Creator Fund, allowing talented creators to earn money directly from their content and build sustainable careers.

2023

Global Expansion

Gramite expanded to 50+ countries and introduced new features including Marketplace, Live Shopping, and enhanced creator tools.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire creativity and bring joy through authentic connections. We believe that everyone has a story to tell and talent to share, and we're building the tools to make that possible.

Our Values

Community First

We prioritize the needs and safety of our community above all else, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone belongs.

Creativity

We celebrate original thinking and provide tools that empower users to express themselves in unique and meaningful ways.

Safety & Privacy

We're committed to protecting our users' data and maintaining a safe platform through advanced technology and human moderation.

Diversity

We embrace and celebrate diverse perspectives, cultures, and backgrounds from around the world.
