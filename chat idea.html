Frequently Asked Questions
How do I send a message?

To send a message on Gramite:

  • Tap the Message icon at the bottom of the screen
  • Tap the + button to start a new conversation
  • Search for the person you want to message
  • Type your message and tap Send
Tip: You can also send photos, videos, and voice messages by tapping the attachment icon.
How do I follow someone?

Following someone on Gramite is easy:

  • Go to the person's profile page
  • Tap the Follow button on their profile
  • You'll start seeing their posts in your feed
Tip: You can unfollow someone at any time by tapping the Following button on their profile.
How do I report a problem?

If you encounter an issue or need to report something:

  • Go to Settings → Report a Problem
  • Select the type of issue you're experiencing
  • Provide a detailed description and any screenshots
  • Our support team will review and respond within 24 hours
Tip: For urgent issues, use the Contact Support option below for faster assistance.
How do I customize my profile?

Customize your profile to make it your own:

  • Go to your Profile page
  • Tap the Edit Profile button (pencil icon)
  • Update your profile picture, bio, and display name
  • Tap Save to apply your changes
Tip: You can also add links to your social media profiles in the edit section.
How do I manage notifications?

Control your notification preferences:

  • Go to Settings → Notifications
  • Toggle notifications on/off for likes, comments, and new followers
  • Choose whether to receive push notifications or email alerts
Tip: You can also mute notifications from specific users by going to their profile and tapping the bell icon.
How do I change my password?

Keep your account secure by updating your password:

  • Go to Settings → Security
  • Tap Change Password
  • Enter your current password and new password
  • Tap Save to confirm the change
Tip: Use a strong password with at least 8 characters, including numbers and special characters.

Need more help?

Our support team is available 24/7 to assist you with any questions or issues.
