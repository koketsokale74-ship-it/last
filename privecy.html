Privacy & Safety Overview

Understanding how Gramite protects your information and ensures a safe experience.

Our Commitment

At Gramite, we believe that privacy and safety are fundamental rights. We are committed to protecting your personal information and creating a safe environment where you can connect, share, and express yourself freely.

Our approach to privacy and safety is built on three core principles:

  • Transparency: We clearly explain how we collect, use, and protect your data.
  • Control: You have control over your privacy settings and what you share.
  • Security: We use industry-leading security measures to protect your information.
🔒 Your Trust Matters: We regularly review and update our privacy and safety practices to ensure they meet the highest standards.
Key Principles
  • Data Minimization: We only collect data that is necessary for providing our services.
  • User Empowerment: You have the right to access, modify, and delete your data.
  • Proactive Safety: We use advanced tools to detect and prevent harmful content.
  • Community Standards: Our guidelines ensure a respectful and inclusive community.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and personalize your experience on Gramite. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, and profile information.
  • Content: Posts, comments, messages, and media you share.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform, including device information and preferences.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we may collect location information to improve your experience.
📌 Important: We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is used solely to improve your Gramite experience.
How We Use Your Data
  • Service Delivery: To provide and maintain our platform features.
  • Improvement: To enhance user experience and develop new features.
  • Communication: To send updates, notifications, and support messages.
  • Safety & Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and harmful content.
Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Restrict: Limit how your data is processed.
  • Object: Object to certain data processing activities.

For any privacy-related requests, contact us at privacy@gramite.com.

Data Protection

How we keep your data secure and what you can do to protect yourself.

Security Measures

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols.
  • Access Control: Strict access controls ensure only authorized personnel can access user data.
  • Monitoring: Continuous monitoring for suspicious activity and potential breaches.
  • Regular Audits: Independent security audits to identify and address vulnerabilities.
🔐 Data Storage: Your data is stored in secure, redundant facilities with strict physical security measures.
What You Can Do
  • Strong Passwords: Use unique, complex passwords for your account.
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Enable 2FA for an extra layer of security.
  • Regular Updates: Keep your app and devices updated with the latest security patches.
  • Privacy Settings: Review and adjust your privacy preferences regularly.

Reporting & Support

How to report issues and get help when you need it.

Report a Problem

If you encounter any of the following, please report it immediately:

  • Harassment or Bullying: Persistent negative behavior directed at you or others.
  • Hate Speech: Content that attacks or discriminates against individuals or groups.
  • Inappropriate Content: Explicit, violent, or otherwise unsuitable content.
  • Impersonation: Accounts pretending to be someone else.
  • Security Issues: Suspicious activity or potential security breaches.
📢 We're Here to Help: Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours and takes appropriate action.
Support Resources
  • Help Center: Visit our Help Center for FAQs and guides.
  • Email Support: support@gramite.com
  • Emergency Contact: If you're in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services.

Account Security

Protect your account with these essential security features.

Security Features
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Add an extra layer of security by requiring a verification code in addition to your password.
  • Password Manager: Use a trusted password manager to create and store strong, unique passwords.
  • Login Notifications: Get alerts when your account is accessed from a new device or location.
  • Session Management: View and manage all active sessions from your account settings.
🛡️ Pro Tip: Enable 2FA and review your active sessions regularly to keep your account secure.
Signs of a Compromised Account
  • Unauthorized Activity: Posts or messages you didn't create.
  • Password Changes: You receive notifications about password changes you didn't make.
  • Login Alerts: Unusual login attempts from unfamiliar locations.
  • Profile Changes: Your profile information has been altered without your knowledge.

If you notice any of these signs, immediately change your password and contact our support team.

Child Safety

Our commitment to protecting minors on Gramite.

Protecting Young Users

We are committed to providing a safe experience for younger users. Our platform is designed with the following protections:

  • Age Requirements: Users must be at least 13 years old to create an account.
  • Parental Controls: Tools for parents to monitor and manage their child's account.
  • Content Filters: Advanced filters to prevent exposure to inappropriate content.
  • Reporting Tools: Dedicated reporting channels for safety concerns involving minors.
👨‍👩‍👦 Parental Guidance: We encourage parents to discuss online safety with their children and use our parental control features.
Tips for Parents
  • Stay Involved: Engage with your child's online activities and know who they interact with.
  • Set Boundaries: Establish clear rules about screen time and online behavior.
  • Educate: Teach your child about online safety, privacy, and the importance of respectful communication.
  • Communicate: Encourage open dialogue about their online experiences and concerns.
  • Use Tools: Take advantage of parental controls and monitoring features available on the platform.
